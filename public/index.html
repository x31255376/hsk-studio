Prepare for the HSK and HSKK, explained in English.

The HSK is the official Chinese proficiency test for non-native speakers, used for university admission, scholarships and work in China. The HSKK is its speaking test. Find your level, learn the words, and rehearse the speaking tasks out loud.

What is changing with HSK 3.0

The new framework has nine bands: 1–3 beginner, 4–6 intermediate, 7–9 advanced. HSK 7–9 already runs as one combined exam with translation and speaking. For levels 1–6, the new format was trialled worldwide in January 2026, and candidates for levels 3–6 in that trial also had to register for a speaking test. Many centres still run the familiar format through 2026, so check your centre on chinesetest.cn before you book.
